## English

### Scope

`data/war_participation_variables_v8.xlsx` is an **author-created, country-level derived analytical dataset** constructed by integrating, aggregating, transforming, cross-checking, and manually coding information from multiple sources.

The workbook is not a copy of the original UCDP/PRIO or Correlates of War datasets and does not reproduce their original conflict-year, state-year, or war-participant tables as distributed by the source providers. Nevertheless, some variables are derived from third-party source data and remain subject to the rights, attribution requirements, and terms applicable to those sources.

### UCDP/PRIO

UCDP states that its datasets are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)** and may be used and redistributed with appropriate citation.

Source and licensing information:  
https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/

The analysis in this repository uses UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset **v25.1** for the principal conflict series and an older historical version for selected location information, as documented in `SOURCES.md` and the workbook legends.

### Correlates of War (COW)

Several variables in the analytical workbook are derived from Correlates of War sources, including State System Membership, National Material Capabilities, Inter-State War Data, and an Intra-State War cross-check.

The repository does **not** include the original COW datasets in their distributed row-level form. Users requiring the original COW datasets should obtain them directly from the Correlates of War Project and comply with its current Terms and Conditions.

COW data and Terms and Conditions:  
https://correlatesofwar.org/data-sets/

The presence of derived COW-based variables in this analytical workbook does not grant users additional rights to the underlying COW source datasets.

### CIA World Factbook and other sources

Selected current military and service variables were constructed from the 2025 edition of the CIA World Factbook. State-identification information also draws on the Gleditsch-Ward state list. Full provenance and citations are provided in `SOURCES.md`.

### Repository license

The repository's **MIT License applies only to the original source code created for this project**. It does not apply to, relicense, or supersede third-party source data and their terms.

No blanket open-data license is asserted for the entire Excel workbook. The workbook is provided as a scholarly replication artifact with source-specific provenance and restrictions retained.

This notice documents provenance and intended reuse conditions; users remain responsible for complying with the terms of the original data providers.
